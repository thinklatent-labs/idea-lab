Noise control

  • Same signal re-fires at most once per 6 hours while open.
  • Quiet hours 20:00 to 07:00 hold Warnings; Criticals always go through.
  • A signal that fires more than 4 times a month without action is flagged for a threshold review on the record.
  • Alerts are measured: time to acknowledge, time to resolve, and the share that led to a change.
Thresholds are evaluated on the same derived metrics the pages show, over the window the record names; an alert links to the page and the moment that caused it. Change a threshold (needs an approval)
