How to read the list

Meets, or holds
The score is at or over the threshold. Where its range reaches the other side, the row says so. Too close to call
The score is on the wrong side, and its range reaches the threshold. Such a model is never named as enough. Under, or over
The whole range is on the wrong side. Too few questions
Under 100 scored questions on that pillar, so there is no score to hold to a threshold.
Not on this page: speed when the endpoint is busy, how long a request a model can take, and whether a model is approved for your data. Those are on each model's card. The preference rating from people's votes is never a threshold.
