7 · An outside address is refused

And can be asked for

An address outside the Enterprise is never added, and nothing is sent to it. You can ask ThinkLatent for it: the ask is a request you follow on Requests, and an outside tool that is allowed is offered to everyone.

This address is outside the Enterprise
It was not added. ThinkLatent decides which outside tools are allowed, one at a time.
It becomes an ask on Requests, with a first answer within 3 days and a reason either way.

8 · Disconnecting, and removing your own

Both can be undone

Disconnecting throws your pass away and leaves the connector in the list. Removing one of your own takes its address and what you supplied. Either can be undone for 10 seconds; steps already listed in your sessions stay.
