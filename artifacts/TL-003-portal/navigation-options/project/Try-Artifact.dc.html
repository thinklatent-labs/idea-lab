1 · A larger piece of work opens beside the chat

The chat narrows · your sessions fold away to give it room

When an answer is a piece of work in itself (a document, a table with a chart, a small page, a diagram, code), it opens in a pane beside the chat and the answer points to it. You can read it, ask for a change, change it by hand, and take it with you.

3 · Its versions

Nothing is overwritten

Each change you ask for, or make by hand, is a new version. Any earlier one can be read again or brought back as the newest.
