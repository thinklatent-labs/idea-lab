Adding a feature

It starts as a sentence and becomes an increment
1 · Say what it should doIn plain words, as for the three above. Jarvis asks what it needs to know: who uses it, how often, how fast it must answer.
2 · Jarvis proposes the modelsFrom the catalogue, with what each has scored and where else it runs. If nothing fits, it becomes an ask to ThinkLatent and you can follow it in Requests.
3 · It goes on the PlanAs an increment in Build, with the five pieces of evidence it will need before Pilot.
A feature can be paused or retired by an owner. Its record, evidence and traces stay.
