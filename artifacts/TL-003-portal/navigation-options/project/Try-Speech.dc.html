1 · Speak a message

Listening, the words in the box, stop, send

What you say is written into the box as you say it. Nothing is sent until you press Send, so every word can be changed first. A spoken message is kept as its words and looks like any other message.

Listening

"Speak" becomes "Stop listening". The words arrive a moment behind your voice; the newest are grey until they settle.

Stopped

It stops when you press Stop, or after 5 seconds of silence. The words are yours to change. "Speak" again adds to them.

Sent like a typed message, after the same check for client data. No sound was kept.

You can speak while an answer is being written: the words wait in the box, as typed words do. One spoken message can run for 2 minutes, a sample value.

2 · An answer, read aloud

Play, pause, speed

"Read aloud" is on every answer. It reads the answer's words and marks the sentence it is on. It says what a table or a piece of code is, without reading it out cell by cell.

The three funds charge 0.12%, 0.45% and 0.80% a year. On 10,000 invested for ten years at the same growth, the dearest costs about seven times what the cheapest does. I have put the comparison in a table beside this chat.

Only one answer is read at a time. Starting another stops the first. Leaving the page stops the reading.

5 · What is kept

The words, never the sound

Speaking changes nothing about what is kept. The words are part of the session, as if typed. The sound is used to make them and then dropped.

Your words
Kept as the message, with the session, for its 30 days
Your voice
Not kept. Dropped as soon as the words are written
The reading
Made when you press play. Not kept
Training
Never used, words or sound
What is counted
The seconds listened to and read, for the model's usage. Never the words
Your limit
Speaking and reading aloud are not requests. Only the message you send counts

6 · The check on spoken words

The same check, at the same moment

Spoken words are checked for client data when you send, exactly as typed words are. A stopped message reaches no model and nothing of it is kept.

Compare the charges for [a client's name], account [a number]
Stopped before it was sent
What you said looks like a client's name with an account number. Nothing was sent to the model and nothing was kept.
Why

While you speak, the sound goes to ThinkLatent's speech model inside the Enterprise and is dropped once written. If you clear the box, nothing remains.

7 · When the microphone is not allowed

Typing always works

The browser decides whether a page may use the microphone. The page says plainly where it stands and how to change it. It listens only while "Listening" shows.

Your browser will ask to use the microphone
Say yes to speak. It is used only while "Listening" shows, and you can take the yes back in the browser at any time.
The browser has blocked the microphone for the portal
"Speak" is off. To allow it, open the padlock beside the address, set Microphone to Allow, then press "Speak" again.

8 · When speech does not work

Nothing is lost, nothing is sent

A failure leaves the box as it was. Whatever was already written down stays in it.

No microphone was found
Plug one in or choose one in your computer's sound settings, then press "Speak" again.
That could not be written down
ThinkLatent's speech model did not answer. The words up to that point are in the box. Type the rest, or try again.
