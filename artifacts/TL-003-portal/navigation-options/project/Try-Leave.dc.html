1 · Download a session

"Download", open · from the session's More menu

A session of yours can be downloaded as text or as a page, with or without its steps and files. It is your copy from then on: the session itself still goes after its 30 days.

3 · Hand a session to a project

"Hand this session to a project", open · it becomes a request

When something works, it can start a prompt in a project. You choose what goes. A copy goes, your session stays yours. The project's owner must accept it, and from then on the copy lives under that project's rules.

5 · The request, as a project's owner sees it

Accepting is one-way, so it is held

An owner sees everything that would come before deciding. Once accepted, the copy is the project's and the person who sent it can no longer withdraw it. So accept and send back are each "hold to".

6 · Copy an answer

"Copy", under every answer

Copy takes the words of one answer and nothing else: no steps, no files, no session. Where you paste them is yours to judge, and the Enterprise's rules on what may leave still hold.

8 · A shared session's link

It opens only for the people named

Sharing a session does not send it anywhere. The link is an address inside the portal. It opens for the people you named, signed in at the Enterprise, and for nobody else.

[the portal's address]/try/shared/fees-explained…

Shared with Dev R. until 5 Dec · Dev can read it, and cannot change it or share it further.

This session has not been shared with youWhat anyone else at the Enterprise sees on opening the link. Outside the Enterprise, the portal's sign-in is all that shows.

9 · What never leaves

Not offered, by design

Nothing in Try TLM is sent outside the Enterprise, by you or by an agent. These are the ways out that tools of this kind usually have. None of them is here.

A link anyone can open, or a code to scanSharing is with named people at the Enterprise onlyNot offered Sending a session or an answer by mailResults and notices stay in the portal's inboxNot offered Answering in a chat channel outside the portalNot offered Saving to storage outside the EnterpriseA download goes to your own computer and nowhere elseNot offered Bringing in sessions from another assistantNothing comes in that way eitherNot offered A session as a pictureText or a page only, so what leaves can be read and searchedNot offered
