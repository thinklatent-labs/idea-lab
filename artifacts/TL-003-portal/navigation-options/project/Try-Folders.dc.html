2 · Make a folder

"New folder", open

A folder needs only a name. Instructions and files can come later. It is yours alone; a folder cannot be shared, though a session in it can be, with people you name.

3 · Its instructions

"Edit", open

What every session in the folder is told before you speak. A change applies from the next message in each session; answers already written stay as they are. The check for client data runs when you save.

28 tokens of the context in every session here. ThinkLatent sets how long instructions may be.

4 · Its files

"Add a file": three states

Every session in the folder can search its files; they are searched, not sent whole. Each file is checked for client data when it is added, and a refused file is not kept. A folder holds 50 files at most.

5 · Move a session in or out

A session's "More" menu

A session is in one folder or in none. It takes the folder's instructions and files from its next message. Moving it does not change its 30 days, and can be undone.

7 · After 30 days

Sessions go, the folder stays

A session in a folder ends 30 days after its last message, like any other. The folder, its instructions and its files stay until you remove them, ready for the next session.

8 · Remove a folder

Held, because it cannot be taken back

Removing a folder removes the folder and its instructions for good, so it is held. Nothing else is lost with it.

9 · A folder is not a project

Two words, two things

Elsewhere these are called projects. In the portal a project is a team's work, so here they are folders.

A folderYours alone, in Try TLM. Groups your own sessions and gives them instructions and files. No client data.
A projectA team's work in the portal, with members, an endpoint of its own and its own rules for data.
