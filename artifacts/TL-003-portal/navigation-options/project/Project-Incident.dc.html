What happened

For 11 minutes at the morning peak, Call summary answered almost nothing. Advisors saw "The summary is not ready yet". Each summary was made again once the service was back, so none was lost.

Why

Replica 2 stopped taking new work when its cache filled. That left one replica for the busiest minutes of the week, 18% above the highest seen before.

What was ruled out

The model and the application: neither changed that week. The gateway: healthy throughout.

What changed since

A third replica, from 24 Oct. The SRE agent now reads time in the queue every 15 seconds.

Still open

Next best action in Production will add about 5k requests a day. A fourth replica for weekday mornings is proposed and waits for Arjun K.
