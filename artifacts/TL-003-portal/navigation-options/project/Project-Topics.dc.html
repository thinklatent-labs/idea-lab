The floorA topic is shown only when at least 200 calls from at least 20 advisers fall in it within the window. Under that, a topic could point at a person or a client, so it is counted and not named. The floor is ThinkLatent's to set, not the project's.
How a topic gets its nameThe topic step on each trace names what the call is about after personal details are removed, and says how sure it is. The watch agent groups those into clusters and proposes a name; a person at ThinkLatent checks a name before it is first shown.
What is never hereNo call, no summary, no quote and no example. Text stays sealed on the trace, and reading it is asked for in the Enterprise's access management, not in the portal (D-293, D-324).
