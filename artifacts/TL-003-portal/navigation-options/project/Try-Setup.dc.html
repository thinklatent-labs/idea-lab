1 · The set-up bar

Above every session

Five things decide how a session answers: the model, an agent if you use one, the tools, the system prompt, and saved settings. Each opens the menu drawn on this board.

Changing any of them applies from the next message; the session keeps what was used for each answer, and says so under it.

2 · Choose a model

Only released models are listed, each with what it is for and its TL Index score. A model whose shared endpoint is busy says so. One answers at a time.

7 · Ask a second model the same thing

Two answers side by side, then you keep one

The same message goes to a second model and the two answers stand side by side, each with its time and what it used. It counts as two requests. You keep one, and the session carries on with that model alone: one model at a time.

Core 4.2 · 6.2 seconds · 4 steps, 2 toolsThe three funds charge 0.12%, 0.45% and 0.80% a year. On 10,000 invested for ten years at the same growth, the dearest costs about seven times what the cheapest does.
Distil 4.2 · 1.9 seconds · no toolsFund A is cheapest at 0.12% a year, fund C dearest at 0.80%. Over ten years the gap adds up to several hundred on 10,000.

Both answers are sample answers on made-up funds. The one you do not keep stays in the session, folded, so you can open it again.
