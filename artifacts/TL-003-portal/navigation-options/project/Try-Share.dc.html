3 · Stopping

At once, with Undo

You can stop sharing with one person or with everyone at any moment. It also ends by itself when the session is removed, or on the day you picked. A session someone started from yours stays theirs.

8 · Sharing an agent or a skill

"Share with people you name", open on Research helper

An agent or a skill is shared the same way as a session, with one more choice: use, or use and change. It stays shared until you stop. Whoever uses it works with their own access, never yours.

9 · What is not offered

Left out on purpose

Other chat tools share by link. Try TLM does not, anywhere. To reach everyone, an agent or a prompt is offered to its library and ThinkLatent accepts it.

A link that opens for whoever has itNot offered · inside or outside the Enterprise A code to scanNot offered · it is a link by another name Sharing with a group, a team or a roleNot offered · name each person A switch that shares with everyoneNot offered · offer it to the Agents library or the prompt library Sharing with someone outside the EnterpriseNot offered Letting a reader write in your sessionNot offered · they start their own Sharing a connector, your memory or a folderNot offered · each person connects, remembers and files for themselves
