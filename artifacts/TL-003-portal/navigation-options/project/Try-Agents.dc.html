4 · Offering one to the Agents library

"Offer it to the Agents library", open on Research helper · step by step

The only way an agent reaches everyone. You offer one version; ThinkLatent checks it and accepts it or sends it back with a reason. There is no switch that opens an agent to everyone.

What you are offering
Research helper · version 4 · saved today 11:20 · Core 4.2
Later saves are not part of this offer.
What goes with it
Its instructions, starter questions and settingsGoes The tools it may useFund documents search · Calculator · Code sandbox · all three are ThinkLatent'sGoes Your sessions, your memory, your sign-ins to connectorsNever goes
You can withdraw the offer until ThinkLatent answers.
1
You offer one version
You say what it is for, who should use it and what it must not be used for. What you wrote is shown to whoever checks it.
2
ThinkLatent checks it
  • It does what it says, on ThinkLatent's own test tasks
  • No client data and no real names in its instructions, files or starter questions
  • Every tool it uses is one everyone may use; a connector works with each user's own access
  • It asks before it changes anything
  • Nothing that does the same is already in the library
3
It becomes a request you can follow
Listed on Requests under "Asks to ThinkLatent", with a first answer within 3 days. The agent shows "Offered" here until then.
4
Accepted, or sent back
Accepted: it appears in the Agents library with you named as its maker. Sent back: the reason comes with it, and you can change the agent and offer it again.
Your own copy
Stays yours and private the whole time. You can keep using and changing it; your changes are not part of the offer. To change what the library holds, offer a newer version.
