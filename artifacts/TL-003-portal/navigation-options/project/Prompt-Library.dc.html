How the library works

Starting from a templateCopy it into your application, fill the highlighted parts and register your version with the template's name. From then on your feature's Prompts page shows which template version you started from.
When a template movesEvery feature that started from it is told, with what changed. Nothing in your application changes by itself: the text lives in your code, and taking the change is a new version of yours.
Offering oneA project owner offers a prompt that has worked. ThinkLatent strips anything particular to the project, runs the eight checks and scores it on its pillar. Then it joins, with your team named.
