For the pillar council · 12 Nov

Index agent
The agent proposes: join Client care, with at most 5 real calls among each edition's 20 new questions.
  • Why a cap: no outside model may be sent a real call. With it, an edition never holds more than 60 such questions of 240, so outside models are still scored on 180 or more, with the count shown. Without it, they would soon have too few to score.
  • The pillar's written questions then last 6 editions, not 4. Writers are still needed: the ask on Upkeep stands.
  • Domain-Advice v2 was trained on this project's calls, so it and its Advisor desktop version are not scored on these. Their Client care score rests on the other questions, and says so.
  • The 16 answers the practitioners changed go back to the project, with their version.
