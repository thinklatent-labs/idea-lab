How the rating is made

Every pair is used at once to place the models on one scale. A longer or smoother answer gets no credit for that alone. The interval says how far the rating would move with other voters.

A pair needs 50 votes before it counts. Vendor B · large against Core 3.4 has 31, the thinnest.
