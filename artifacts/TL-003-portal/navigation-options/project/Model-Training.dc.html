When it ends

The same four steps every time · nothing reaches a project until it is released
1 · The TL Index is runThe whole of edition 2026-11, beside 4.2 at 81 ± 1. If it is not better where it aimed and level elsewhere, it stops here. The foundation lead confirms it works.
2 · It is releasedCopied to the released models with its record, and listed in the catalogue. Each project on Core or a model built on it is told a new version is ready.
3 · Each project's evaluation is run for itThe portal runs its evaluation set, its judges and its self-healing benchmark on the released version, so the results are waiting. Where a project has gains of its own, they are re-applied first.
4 · Each project decidesWith its own results in front of it. A switch travels as an increment with a canary, on its own change request. A project may also stay where it is.
