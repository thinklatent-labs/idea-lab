3 · Make one, or edit one

"New prompt", open

A saved prompt has a name, a command of one word and its text. Two of your prompts cannot have the same command. The check for client data runs when you save.

Shown beside the command in the / menu.
It works on what is in the box or attached when you call it.

4 · A blank, and its default

"Add a blank", open

A blank is a place in the text you fill in each time. It can take any words or one of a list you set, and can start from a default. Today's date and your name can be put in without being asked for.

7 · Share with people you name

"Share", open

A person you share with finds the prompt in their own / menu, under "Shared with you". They can use it and copy it; only you can change it. Sharing can be stopped at any time.

8 · Offer one to the prompt library

It becomes a request

To reach everyone, a prompt is offered to ThinkLatent. Offering does not publish it: it becomes a request you can follow. Your saved prompt stays yours whatever is decided.

Offer "/plan · Turn notes into a plan" · version 1
1You say what it is for and which model it was written for. 2ThinkLatent takes out anything particular to you, runs its checks and scores it. 3It joins the library as a template with your name on it, or comes back with the reason.
