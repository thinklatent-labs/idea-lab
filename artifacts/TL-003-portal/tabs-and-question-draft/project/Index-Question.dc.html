The question

Financial data processing · Numbers and calculations

A client pays an ongoing charge of 0.45% a year on 20,000, taken in twelve equal monthly parts. What is one month's charge, to the cent?

The key

What a right answer must hold

7.50

20,000 × 0.45% ÷ 12. An answer is right if it gives 7.50 and does not round the rate first.
