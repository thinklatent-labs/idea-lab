Grace policy

The portal earns compliance before it enforces it. Enforcement steps are dated on the record and visible to the team in advance.
  1. Day 0Seen. The likely team is told what the portal saw and offered the assistant or a blueprint; nothing changes for them.
  2. Day 14Tags required. Calls without use_case and stage get a warning header; the digest names the share.
  3. Day 30Record required. Unregistered keys lose production endpoints and keep the sandbox; the owner is told a week ahead.
  4. Vendor directApproved vendors stay reachable; the team is shown the same blueprint with the vendor behind the alias, so tracking costs them nothing. Unapproved hosts go to Risk the day they appear.
Policy approved by the Head of ThinkLatent on 2026-11-02; Risk concurrence pending. Enforcement dates and exceptions are approvals on the record; ThinkLatent cannot block a key on its own.
Sources: gateway request log (key, alias, tags, prompt_id); egress proxy logs by destination host and network zone; the record store. Volumes only, never content. Adoption funnel on Use cases
