The loop

  1. 1 · Done Mine tasks 4 tasks found behind one model; this one chosen.
  2. 2 · Waiting Gap analysis Needs the index line item for this task; self-healing reads the gap report from it.
  3. 3 · Not ready Train a specialist Unlocks at [target] eligible samples; estimated [n] hours once it does. Train as generate, extract (structured) or decide (a calibrated choice); decision specialists report held-out accuracy and calibration error before and after. Registers call-summary-specialist v1.
  4. 4 · Not ready Evaluate offline Specialist against incumbent and teacher on the line item, Official run; the regression gate decides. Pass sets alias candidate.
  5. 5 · Not ready Canary 5%, then 50%, then 100%, each step approved here with the judgement from the Models page. Alias canary carries the weight.
  6. 6 · Not ready Migrate Alias prod moves to the specialist; the incumbent keeps alias previous for 30 days, then retires; the record is updated.
Aliases, not names, move traffic. prod · canary · candidate · previous — each points at one registered version; every move is a dated row on the record. Rollback is "prod ← previous" and needs no approval.
