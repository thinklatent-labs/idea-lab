What it does

1Ingest: clean and chunk documents (PDF, DOCX, HTML); PII check before anything is stored
2Retrieve: hybrid search over the team's store, inside the boundary
3Answer: TLM4 Core (or Claude or Gemini where approved) with citations
4Check: groundedness judge on every answer; low scores abstain
5Learn: feedback and corrections go to the project's data set; self-healing fills gaps
