Under review · when it applies
When the next review falls due, after a material change (model, data boundary or risk tier), or when a Critical finding stays open 30 days. The use case keeps serving; the regression suite re-runs and the owner re-attests before it returns to Production.
Review cadence by risk tier: tier 4 every 3 months · tier 3 every 6 · tier 2 every 12 · tier 1 every 24
Decommissioned · how it ends
An owner-signed decommission plan: dependents told 30 days ahead, aliases removed, keys revoked, and the record and audit trail kept for 7 years. Data sets are kept or deleted per the record's retention, which the owner re-attests every year.
