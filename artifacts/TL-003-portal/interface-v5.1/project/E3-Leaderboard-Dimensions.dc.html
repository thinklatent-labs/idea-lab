Accuracy and hallucination, scored apart

Accuracy is the share of answers fully correct; hallucination is the share with an invented or unsupported claim. The adjusted score counts a correct answer +1, an abstention or escalation 0 and a hallucinated answer −1, so a model that declines when unsure never ranks below one that guesses wrong.
