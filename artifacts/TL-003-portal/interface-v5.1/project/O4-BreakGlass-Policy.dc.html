The rule · v4, proposed 5 Nov · pending Risk concurrence
A break-glass grant is a time-boxed IAM role. The reason is recorded when it is requested and reviewed after it ends. Active grants show on the page they open.
Time box
1 hour at most · no renewal without a new approval
Approver
The Head of ThinkLatent only; no delegation
Scope
One use case's dedicated endpoint, one environment, named actions
Review
Within 5 working days by someone who was not the approver
Visibility
An active-now chip on the page the grant opens, with who and why
Sealed data
Never a route to production request content; aggregates only
Audit
Every grant, review and finding is in the audit export with its evidence id
