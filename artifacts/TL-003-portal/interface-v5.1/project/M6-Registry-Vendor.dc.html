Evidence that expires, and the re-runs scheduled

The rule, and the Official runs booked to keep evidence current.
Governance portfolio
Vendor evidence expires 90 days after its Official run, because a vendor can change the model behind its API without notice. A re-run is scheduled 14 days before expiry. Once evidence expires the scores show a dash, the model takes no new use cases, and existing use cases keep running with a warning on their record. Open-weight evidence does not expire while the weights match their pinned hash; new weights register a new version.
