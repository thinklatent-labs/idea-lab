Steps 1 to 3 done
1 · Intent · Priya N., 30 Oct

Pre-review outgoing client letters and secure-message replies for compliance issues before a person approves them. Clean drafts go to a fast lane; anything flagged goes to a Compliance reviewer with the rule that applies. The model never sends or approves a letter.

Volume
About 120 000 drafts a month across Client Services (gateway aggregate; production content stays sealed)
Who uses it
Client Services writers, and the Compliance communications reviewers who approve flagged drafts
Out of scope
Marketing material and anything published outside the Enterprise; final approval always stays with a person
2 · Requirements · agreed with the Compliance communications lead, 30 Oct
RequirementBarWhy

R2 Clean drafts passed without a flag≥ 0.80Below this the fast lane saves reviewers no time
R3 Rule cited correctly when flagged≥ 0.90Reviewers act on the cited rule
R4 Cost per draft≤ $0.002About $240 a month at current volume

Hosting and service level—Dedicated production endpoint; 99.9% availability and the use case's own time-to-first-token target (targets, set at onboarding)
