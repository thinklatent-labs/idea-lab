Versions and the version contract

Adopters follow the prod alias or pin a version. A new version is re-evaluated and approved before it takes the prod alias, and adopters are told a week ahead.
See the 2.4 notice
1 · Follow or pin

Follow the prod alias, the default, to move with each approved version; or pin one, such as knowledge-agent:2.3, and move when you choose. 7 projects follow; 2 are pinned to 2.2.

2 · Re-evaluated and approved first

A version takes the prod alias only after an Official run on the library suite and on each following adopter's eval set, and the validator's approval. Approval for 2.4

3 · Told a week ahead

Following projects get an inbox notice and an email seven days before the prod alias moves, with the results and how to pin instead. Inbox
