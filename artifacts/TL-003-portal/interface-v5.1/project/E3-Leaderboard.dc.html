Limitations

Read before citing a rank.
Ranks are not totals
A tie means the evidence cannot separate the models at 95%; do not cite "second" for a model in a 2–3 tie.
The judge is a model
Evaluator v2.3 agrees with people at kappa 0.78 on the advice domain; agreement on Client care is lower (0.69 on the Index, below the 0.70 floor; finding F-0421 open).
Entity recognition calibration is stale
The judge has not been recalibrated on Entity recognition for edition 2026-11; the column stays greyed until 40 labels land (due 9 Nov).
Cost is measured, not listed
TLM cost comes from the gateway serving log at the pinned serving config S-11; the frontier API figure is its published rate at measured token counts and excludes contract discounts.
Safety and grounding is under correction
Five reference answers are being re-labelled (CR-0042); Safety and grounding scores may move inside their intervals.
Too few is not a fail
Cells under 100 scored items show "too few": the frontier API on Compliance (only 60 items may leave the Enterprise) and Open-source 70B on Entity recognition (long inputs errored). They are neither cleared nor failed.
Practice rows are not evidence
They show direction for the team only and never appear on a record, the Index or external material.
