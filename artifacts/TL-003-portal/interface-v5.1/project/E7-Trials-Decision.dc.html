Question
Can TLM4 Distil 4.2 pre-review outgoing client correspondence for compliance issues well enough to fast-track clean drafts?
Answer
Not alone. It misses the catch-rate bar (0.91 ± 0.02 against 0.95) and the rule-citation bar (0.86 ± 0.03 against 0.90), mostly on complex-product risk statements (S3) and recommendations phrased as information (S6). It ties the clean-draft bar.

Recommendation
A now and B in parallel. Move to the adapter only after a re-trial on a fresh rotation clears all five requirements.

Risks
Scenarios stop at rulebook 2026-07, so a rule change needs a scenario refresh. Core 4.2 at $490 a month is over the limit until B lands or the owner raises it.
