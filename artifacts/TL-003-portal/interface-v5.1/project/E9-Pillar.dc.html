What this pillar measures

The definition the Index scores against, agreed by the pillar's business owner and the ThinkLatent validators.
Live in the MVP

Client care measures whether a model handles a client's request the way a good Enterprise service representative would: it understands what the client needs, sends it to the right team, flags a complaint or a sign that the client may need extra care, and drafts a reply or case note that is accurate, grounded in Enterprise policy and written in plain, respectful language. It does not score investment reasoning (2 Financial analysis and reasoning) or regulatory wording (4 Compliance and regulatory); those have pillars of their own.
