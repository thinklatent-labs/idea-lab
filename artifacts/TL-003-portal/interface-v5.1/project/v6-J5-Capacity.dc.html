What it means for capacity

About two inputs in five skip most of the prefill, mainly the shared instructions and earlier turns of a thread. A higher hit rate would lower the weekday peaks without adding GPUs, which is why the scale-out proposal is reversible: if a cache change lifts the rate, the added GPU may not be needed.

Hit rate did not fall in the busiest weeks, so the cache is not being squeezed out by load yet.
