# ThinkLatent Portal (portal) - UI evaluation 2026-11-18

Version 1.0 · 18 Nov 2026 · Lab session TL-003 portal · Critic · Part 1 of the UI evaluation (nothing was changed)

The locked build (branch `ui-lock-2026-11-17`, integration 309ac57a) was reviewed in light and dark by six independent reviewers, one per page group, each using design critique, the accessibility review and the ThinkLatent web design checklist. They reported 232 findings; merged, that is 146 rows: 3 critical, 84 major, 59 minor. 31 rows combine one finding reported by several groups.

The table Jithin marks agree or disagree is the Sheet: https://claude.ai/artifact/SmNFR9MGZMviBDeu4rvz4x

## Jithin's ten points

| No. | Your point | Verdict | Evidence | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Green gradient is mixed with something else | Confirmed by all six | Three hues: yellow-green (about 107 to 117 degrees), brand green (135), teal (150 to 180). The stylesheet mixes the lit green with the kit's 'companion' teal. | One brand-green field, kept out from under text. |
| 2 | Dark-mode cards could be white | Partly: the problem is real, the fix differs | Cards are 1.03 to 1.06:1 against black. | 3 | Colour of the buttons | Confirmed by all six | Three fills carry five meanings; primary follows position; in dark the primary and 'selected' are the same pale pill. | Brand green fill for the one primary action only. |
| 4 | What is a link is not evident | Confirmed by all six | 96 of 231 links unmarked on shell pages, 45 of 75 on Index pages, 25 of 26 on the chat; five link forms. | One link style; whole rows and tiles as targets. |
| 5 | Hierarchical grouping is lacking | Confirmed on most pages | No hero on Home, Value, Index first page, model, project and feature Overviews; equal-weight cards; weak surface separation. Not on Settings, Inbox or My work's Overview. | One hero per page; group by relationship; fold the rest. |
| 6 | Too much written information | Confirmed by all six | 17,539 words across Try TLM's 20 pages; 1,200 to 2,200 on the heaviest pages elsewhere. | A text budget per section; info tips for method. |
| 7 | Hard to see how to operate | Confirmed by all six | Unlabelled dock, moving tab row and actions, writing box off the first screen, blank default window, scope menus that do not switch. | Stable object head; labelled nav; controls where the task starts. |

## What was not checked

| What was not checked | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Screen readers | None was run; names, roles and landmarks were read from the page structure. |
| Phone | Only sideways overflow at 390 px, plus the Try TLM chat driven by touch emulation. No full phone review. |
| 200% zoom, forced colours, colour-blind simulation | Not run, except overflow at 720 px on the shell pages. |
| Speed and loading states | The build is a local file on sample data, so view-change time, skeletons and real errors were not seen. |
| Pages measured but not looked at as pictures | Other projects' pages (11), other models' tabs, Next best action, three run pages, and the lower parts of most data and training pages. |
| Pages missing from the first capture | Five Index pages, an agent's page and a system page; the reviewers captured them themselves (the system page in light only). |
| Boards | Only a few screens were compared with their boards, so some findings may be things the boards draw. |
| Contrast method | Background is the median pixel behind each text run; values within about 0.1 of the limit are marginal. |
| Counts | 232 findings from six reviewers were merged to 146 rows; 31 rows combine the same finding reported by several groups. |
