# Glass study and colour v5, proposed (23 Nov 2026)

Drawn in the TL-003 portal session for Jithin, Head of ThinkLatent, from his reference of a dark page of frosted-glass cards. A drawing for discussion: nothing here is in the design kit yet (decision D-467).

- `glass-dark.jpg`, `glass-light.jpg`: the page head and five surfaces of the reference, rebuilt with the portal's sample data, on a true-black canvas and on the kit's light canvas. Every text was measured at 4.6:1 or better against what is rendered behind it.
- `colour-v5-proposed.jpg`: the Enterprise red measured again (#C20028, with the wordmark's ink #95141D), the glass tints in both modes, and the four buttons Jithin named.
- `study.html`: the one self-contained page the three images were rendered from. Its theme block lists every value as a candidate token.

The five surfaces: (1) tinted glass with capsule rows, (2) quiet glass with pills and value chips, (3) frost glass with a card inside a card, (4) deep glass with a large number and a raised chart panel, (5) tinted tiles around one light.

Why the red changed: the kit's #B2242F was the screenshot's Display P3 numbers used as if they were sRGB. Converted, the red on the Enterprise's site is #C20028.
