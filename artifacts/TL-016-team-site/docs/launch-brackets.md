# ThinkLatent site — launch brackets to fill (TL-016)

Nov 7, 2026 · @Jithin Pradeep

Every value the ThinkLatent site needs before launch that only the team holds. The TPM collects them all by 21 Nov 2026. Each row names who owns the answer; fill in Value, Source and Date, and leave a row empty if the answer isn't known yet.

## How to fill

- **One value per row.** Write it as it should appear on the page, in plain words.
- **Every number needs a source and a date.** Examples: the ThinkLatent Portal registry, serving telemetry, Jira, a survey. A number without a source does not go live.
- **Quotes from partner teams need their written approval.** Note where the approval is kept (an email or a record) in the Source column.
- **Names only with the person's agreement**, especially award citations and profiles.
- **Questions go in comments** on the row, not in the cells.
- Template fields that authors fill when they write a post are not listed here. ThinkLatent Day is left out until the Summit date is set.

The design these brackets fill is the ThinkLatent site canvas, v8 (decision D-137 in the Idea Lab).
