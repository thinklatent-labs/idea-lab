Generations

Each generation, in outcomes

What each one made possible. Techniques live in the engineering series and the portal.

  1. TLM1 · in production

    Before LLMs

    An Enterprise BERT and the speech model that processes the Enterprise's phone calls.

  2. TLM2

    Built in-house

    A 108B sparse mixture-of-experts model, the Enterprise's own.

  3. TLM3

    Smaller and faster

    TLM2 pruned and distilled to 20 to 30B for lower cost and latency.

  4. TLM4 · current

    Core, Distil, Domain

    Base moved to Gemma; Core 27B and 7B on curated Enterprise data; the tier that drives adoption today.

  5. TLM5 · next

    Sees where things are heading

    A text-only large world model that learns from observation and predicts where an interaction is going, and can guide a frontier model from the Enterprise's past interactions.

What the index measures, and why

Three lines on the TL Index

It scores models, ours and others, on the skills the Enterprise needs: financial data, customer-care language, compliance, cybersecurity, safety, entity recognition, judging, coding and agent skills.

Every ThinkLatent release is measured against the last one, in the same way, and the worse results are shown too.

A number without its method is not a number; the index publishes its method, its data and a plain-language explainer with each run.
