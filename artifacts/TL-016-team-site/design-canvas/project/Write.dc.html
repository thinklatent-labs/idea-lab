Perspective

For business readers

What changed for the people who use it, and why it matters at the Enterprise. No architecture, no parameter counts, no benchmark names. If a term needs the glossary, link it; if it needs a paragraph, it belongs in a Deep dive.

Most posts. Every win, release and leadership post is a Perspective.

Deep dive

For practitioners

How it was done, for the people who will build on it: methods, trade-offs, what did not work. Still no client names and nothing unreleased; the editor checks.

Marked Deep dive on the card so a business reader knows before clicking.

How to publish

Three steps

  1. 1

    Start from the template

    In SharePoint: New › News post › pick the template. The category, properties and layout are already set. Draft with AI if you like; you are the author.

  2. 2

    Submit for review

    Save as draft and send the link with the form below. One peer and the editor review within five working days for clarity, anything unreleased, client names and approvals.

  3. 3

    Publish and share

    The editor publishes; it lands on Home, the Blog and the next monthly email automatically, and goes to the intranet and the office news when it fits. Share the link with the people who have the problem.

The ThinkLatent voice

Four rules for everything we write

Gain before build

Lead with what someone can now do, not what we built.

Their problem first

Say it in the reader's words, before ours.

One number

Dated and sourced. No number we cannot back.

End with an invitation

"Do you have this problem? Talk to us."

Instead of

"We fine-tuned a domain model on [N] documents and improved accuracy on our benchmark."

Write

"[Partner team] now [does what] in [time] instead of [time]. If your team [has the same problem], let's talk."
