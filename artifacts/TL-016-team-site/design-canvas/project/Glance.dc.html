Mandate

  • ThinkLatent builds the AI infused with the Enterprise's proprietary data: language, speech, omni and foundation models.
  • Purpose: the Enterprise's own intelligence compounds; vendor models make everyone as good as the vendor, only the Enterprise's models carry its customers' feedback loop.
  • Measured on latency, cost, scale and competitive advantage. Head of ThinkLatent: Jithin Pradeep.
