How the pairs work

Each category has an a and a b, so two posts of one kind can sit side by side in News without repeating. Sessions have four, one per session in a row.

The focus sits inside the centre 4:3 of every thumbnail, so the News web part's 4:3, 16:9 and 21:9 crops and the 16:9 of Highlighted content all keep it.

Product tiles

Six tiles, 1600 × 1200

At 4:3, for Quick links in the Tiles layout and the Hero web part's tiles.

Patterns

Five patterns, optional

At most one a page, behind a section with little text; never behind a hero or the closing band.
