How the blog runs

Researchers write. Three editors keep it moving.

  • Written by the people who did the work; drafted with AI if they like, authored by them
  • An editorial board of two or three, with one function head as advisor; review within five working days
  • At most two posts a month, one leadership post (the four leaders in turn) and one from the team: fewer, better posts beat a feed
  • Every post is a Perspective (outcomes, for business readers) or a Deep dive (technique allowed, for practitioners)
  • Shared to the intranet and the Canada and India office news; the external blog later, through Comms
  • Comments are on, moderated by the editors
