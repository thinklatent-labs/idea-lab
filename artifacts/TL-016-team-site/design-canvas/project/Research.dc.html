Our work · Research

We prove the method before anyone builds on it.

Three research themes, each tied to what it means for the Enterprise, and a joint lab with the university's Department of Computer Science. Research here never touches vendor data.

WinsResearchPublications
Autonomous AI

Building and improving language models

The methods behind TLM: how models are developed, pruned, distilled, self-healed and trained on the Enterprise's data; and when a small model is enough. Includes SLM feasibility, telling a team from its data whether a small model will work before anyone trains it.

What it means for the EnterpriseModels that know the Enterprise's work, at a fraction of frontier cost, and an answer on feasibility before the budget is spent.

Trustworthy AI

Testing AI the way an adversary would, and earning trust

Red-teaming methods and guardrails on the offensive side, including M-cube, a published red-teaming method. On the other side, what makes people trust an AI's help with a decision, how emotion shapes that decision, and how an AI should respond.

What it means for the EnterpriseAI that holds up under attack and under scrutiny, in a firm where trust is the product.

Cybersecurity

AI that defends, and resists other AI

Models and techniques that make infrastructure more robust against attacks, including attacks run by other AIs. Built on TLM Core, reuse evaluated before anything new is trained.

What it means for the EnterpriseDefences that learn as fast as the attacks do.

Partnership

The university lab

A joint initiative with the university's Department of Computer Science, announced in May 2025, on top of a decade of MScAC student projects. Faculty, post-doctoral fellows and students working with ThinkLatent's Canada-based researchers on co-authored papers, seminars and recruitment. This page carries what the lab's own site does not yet: the joint outputs, the interns and the partner stories.
