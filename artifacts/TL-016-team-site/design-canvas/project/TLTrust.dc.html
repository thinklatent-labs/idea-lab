How we build responsibly

How ThinkLatent builds, alongside the Enterprise's model risk management.

ThinkLatent's delivery process supports, and does not replace, the Enterprise's model risk management. These are the steps ThinkLatent takes before and after a model is used in a business process, and who is responsible for each. Approval and validation sit with Risk and the model inventory; we link to them rather than restate them.

The steps

Six steps, and who is responsible

A use case that does not meet a step does not proceed. A model in use that falls below its agreed bar is escalated under the Enterprise's model risk management, which may restrict or suspend its use.

  1. Step 01

    Intake

    A team brings the problem through the form or the portal. ThinkLatent records the business purpose, the data involved, who the model will affect and what it must not do, and the model is registered in the Enterprise's model inventory.

    Responsible: the ThinkLatent product lead with the partner team's owner
    You see: the request, its owner and its stage

  2. Step 02

    Risk classification

    Every use case is referred to Risk for classification before training starts. Risk decides the class and the validation required.

    Responsible: the product lead refers; Risk decides the class
    You see: the class Risk set and its conditions

  3. Step 03

    Developer evaluation

    The model is scored on the TL Index for the skills the use case needs, against the last release and a reference baseline, with weaker results shown too. This is ThinkLatent's own evaluation, not model validation.

    Responsible: the index lead
    You see: the result, its interval and the run, labelled developer evaluation

  4. Step 04

    Independent validation

    Validation under the Enterprise's model risk management, as Risk requires for the use case. ThinkLatent supplies the evidence.

    Responsible: Risk; ThinkLatent does not sign
    You see: the inventory record, linked

  5. Step 05

    Human review

    Reviewers from the partner team check outputs at the level and frequency the approval sets out. Agents propose; people decide.

    Responsible: the partner team's reviewers
    You see: the review's findings and what changed

  6. Step 06

    Monitoring

    Calls through the ThinkLatent gateway are logged as the approval requires; drift, refusals and escalations are watched. A use case below its agreed bar is reported to Risk through the model risk issue process.

    Responsible: AI Products with the use case's owner; periodic revalidation by Risk
    You see: the stage on the use case record

What we show

The control, every time

  • Each product's ThinkLatent process stage, with an as-of date
  • Developer evaluation results for registered models, with interval and run, labelled on every chart
  • What each model must not do, in plain words
  • For approved models only, the limits and use conditions stated in the approval
  • Who to ask, by name, for every product
What we don't

The code, the data, the architecture

  • How a model is built: that belongs in the engineering series and the portal, for the people who build
  • Training data details
  • Client names
  • Approval and validation status: the model inventory holds it, linked below
  • Limits of models still in validation, which stay with their sponsors and Risk
  • Policy text: it is the Enterprise's, and it is linked below
