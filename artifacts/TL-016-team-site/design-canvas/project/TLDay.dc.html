How it runs

Mixed teams, real problems, a working demo

One business partner with two or three ThinkLatent builders per team. The problem comes from the business side; the build runs on ThinkLatent's products; the demo shows what a team can do now.

  1. Before

    Bring a problem

    Business teams sign up with the problem in their words. ThinkLatent matches each with builders and a product: TLM Core or Distil, the portal, or the index.

  2. Day 1

    Build

    Relay endpoints, the playground and a ThinkLatent coach per team. Office hours from Risk for anything client-facing. Nothing touches client data.

  3. Day 2

    Demo

    Eight minutes a team, a live demo in each, one Q&A at the end. Judged by the Head of ThinkLatent and two business leaders on the gain, the number and whether it could ship.

  4. After

    Carry it on

    Winners join the quarter's ThinkLatent awards and are featured on Home. The best idea enters the Idea Lab as a Seed. Every demo is recorded and recapped.
