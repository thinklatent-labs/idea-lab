What this index cannot tell you

  • Whether a model is good at a task the pillars do not cover; a use case's own line item is the evidence for that.
  • Anything about traffic outside the item set's languages, lengths and formats; drift on the Quality page says when production has left the set.
  • Fine differences inside an interval; two models whose intervals overlap are tied here, whatever the ranking order suggests.
  • Judge-scored pillars are only as good as the calibration above; exact-match pillars do not have this problem.
  • Frontier columns are run through the same items from outside the boundary and may be days older than the owned columns.
  • A Practice run proves nothing to Risk; only an Official run is evidence on a record.
