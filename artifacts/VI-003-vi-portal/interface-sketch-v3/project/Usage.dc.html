How cost is counted

Dedicated machines are charged as a fixed monthly share; shared serving by tokens. "Vendor-equivalent avoided" prices the same tokens at the approved vendor's list rate for a comparable model, as agreed with Finance. Both methods are versioned and linked from the record.
